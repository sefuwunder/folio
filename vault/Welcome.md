# Welcome to Folio

A macOS-inspired home for your notes — **markdown**, plain text, and PDFs, organized in folders.

## Try it

- Press `⌘K` (or `/`) to search everything
- Toggle **Edit / Preview** while writing markdown
- Right-click (or long-press) any note or folder for actions
- Drag notes onto folders to move them

## Sync

Open **Sync** in the toolbar to connect your OpenDrive.com account. Folio syncs this whole vault to a remote folder (default `/Folio`) — manual only, conflicts are kept as ` (conflict)` copies, and deletes are never propagated.
